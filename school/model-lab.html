Why the check row always ties. Assets change by exactly what the cash flow statement says they change by: cash moves by free cash flow, working capital by its delta, PP&E by capex minus depreciation, and the sum of those movements is, line for line, net income landing in retained earnings. When your model breaks, one of those 4 joints is where. Ready for real drivers? Switch to Level 2.
