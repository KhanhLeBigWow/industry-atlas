1 · The paper LBO

The classic PE screen: 5 minutes, no Excel, round numbers said aloud. Work it on paper first, then step through the solution. Every "next step" is a line you should be able to narrate.

2 · Returns mental math

MOIC to IRR and back, no calculator: the anchors are 2x in 5 years ≈ 15%, 3x in 5 ≈ 25%, and the rule of 72 for doubling times. Say your answer before revealing.

3 · Accretion / dilution snap call

All-stock deal, no synergies: accretive or dilutive, and roughly how much? The rule: compare the P/Es. The buyer's expensive paper buying cheaper earnings accretes.

4 · The LBO ladder: how the pros stage the climb

Every serious LBO curriculum: from street training programs to PE fund onboarding: stages the same 5-rung climb. Know which rung a given test expects, and practice one rung above it.

The trap: candidates polish rung 1 and jump to rung 5 talk. Tests fail people at rungs 3 and 4: conventions and ties. The Model Lab Level 2 trains exactly those conventions on the 3-statement side.
