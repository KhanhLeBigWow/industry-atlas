D.1 The primer

Two desks share the floor. DCM runs investment-grade issuance: high-volume, low-fee, relationship flow: a treasurer calls, and $2B prices by lunch off a spread to Treasuries. Leveraged finance is the credit engine of the sponsor economy: high-yield bonds and leveraged loans (each a $1.4T+ US market) underwriting buyouts, dividends, and refinancings at real fees and real risk.

The defining fight of this decade: private credit ($2T-class AUM) took the middle of the leveraged market from the syndicated desks: speed and certainty against price and scale: and now the banks partner with, compete against, and lend to the very funds displacing them. Anyone joining LevFin today is joining that turf war. The Private Credit dossier is the other half of this track.

D.4 The landscape

IG DCM: JPMorgan, BofA, Citi lead by flow: balance sheet is the ticket. LevFin: the same names plus Goldman, Barclays, and Jefferies (the leveraged-credit-heavy independent). Across the table: CLOs (the biggest loan buyers), high-yield mutual funds, and increasingly the private-credit giants (Ares, Apollo, Blackstone Credit, Golub) who ARE the market for mid-size deals. Career adjacency runs 2 ways: LevFin to private credit or distressed funds is the classic lateral; the RX desk is where LevFin's mistakes go to be renegotiated.

D.5 The study kit

Credit metrics until they are reflexes (leverage, coverage, FCCR: and the thresholds in the RX category), one credit agreement summary read end to end, this week's spreads (IG, HY, loans: free from index publishers' commentary), and a view on private credit vs syndicated. The interview is metrics fluency plus market levels: sounding current is half the grade.
