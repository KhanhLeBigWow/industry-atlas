1 The map: the book in the middle

Issuers and their owners bring shares (green); investors bring orders (blue); the syndicate desk builds the book and allocates. Gross spreads run ~7% on small IPOs down to ~1-2% at mega-cap, and near-zero on competitive blocks: scale is everything.

3 The roles

On the ECM desk

Analyst→Associate→VP / Syndicate→MD
AnalystMarket update pages, comps at pricing, case studies of every recent deal, dilution math.
AssociateDrafting sessions, roadshow logistics, order-book tracking.
SyndicateThe book itself: price talk, allocations, stabilization: the desk's sharp end.
MDWindow judgment and issuer relationships: when to launch, at what range.

Across the table

PM·Analyst (buy side)·Corp treasurer
Buy-side analystThe 10-day IPO underwrite: model from the S-1, meet management, size the order.
PMDecides play-or-pass and at what limit: anchors negotiate before the range is set.
Treasurer / CFOThe issuer's chair: proceeds, dilution, and which banks earn the mandate.
