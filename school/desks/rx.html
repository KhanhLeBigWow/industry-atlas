Restructuring.

The countercyclical desk: when credit breaks, RX advises the company (debtor side) or its creditors on who owns what now. It is the most technical seat in advisory, the best credit education on the street, and busiest exactly when everything else is quiet.

D.1 The primer

Two chairs at every table: debtor-side (advise the company: liquidity, DIP, the plan) and creditor-side (advise a class: maximize ITS recovery). The work spans out-of-court exchanges and amend-and-extends, liability-management transactions, prepackaged filings, and full Chapter 11s: plus distressed M&A (363 sales) and new-business screening for whoever cracks next.

Why now is a golden age for the desk: a decade of cov-lite documents made liability management (drop-downs, uptiers, double-dips) a standing product; higher-for-longer rates keep a maturity wall rolling toward over-levered 2021 vintages; and private credit's rise means workouts increasingly happen in conference rooms instead of courtrooms. The deep technicals live in the RSSG worked example and the interview bank's RX category (10 questions): this page is the desk around them.

D.4 The landscape

The league: PJT RSSG and Houlihan Lokey trade the crown (Houlihan leads by count, PJT by mega-case presence), with Evercore, Lazard, Moelis, and Perella Weinberg all running serious franchises: debtor mandates pay best; creditor committees train best. Across the table: distressed funds (Oaktree, Apollo hybrid value, Elliott, Silver Point, Davidson Kempner class), CLOs defending their tranches, and the law firms (Kirkland, Weil, Davis Polk class) who co-run every case. The classic exits: distressed credit funds, special situations, or staying to make the uniquely sticky RX partner economics.

D.5 The study kit

Waterfall math until you can find a fulcrum on a napkin, the 13-week's logic, 2 live cases followed via their free dockets (first-day declarations are the best crash courses in finance), and the liability-management case names (J.Crew, Serta, Envision) with what each exploited. Then the 6-week RSSG plan end to end: it was built for exactly this desk.
