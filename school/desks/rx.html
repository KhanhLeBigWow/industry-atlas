2 The waterfall, drawn

Value pours from the top: an illustrative $1.0B EV against $1.4B of claims

100% · cash

100% · new debt
Second-lien notes ($400M)
~75% · the FULCRUM
Unsecured notes ($250M)
~8% · warrants
Equity ($? wiped)
0% · out of the money

The fulcrum class recovers partially and converts into the reorganized equity: it becomes the new owner. Move the EV assumption $150M and the fulcrum moves a class: which is why RX valuation fights are existential, and why every party's "independent" expert lands exactly where their client needs.
