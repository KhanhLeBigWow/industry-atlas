3 The roles: who does what in a deal

On the advisory desk

Analyst→Associate→VP→MD / Partner
AnalystNAV rolls and the pricing model, data-room build, buyer tracking grids, fund cash-flow pacing.
AssociateRuns the process calendar, first-draft materials, manages buyer Q&A.
VPQuarterbacks the auction: bid comparisons (deferred bids restated to PV), GP consent choreography.
MDOwns the LP and GP relationships; sets price talk; wins the next mandate.

At the secondaries fund

Analyst→Associate→Principal→Partner / IC
AnalystBottoms-up NAV work: roll every company in every fund, run pacing scenarios, screen data rooms.
AssociateUnderwrites the book: price-to-return solves, GP quality scoring, reference calls.
PrincipalStructures the bid: deferrals, strips, syndication; negotiates docs.
Partner / ICPortfolio construction and the final price: which GPs to be long, forever.

4 The technicals: 5 builds, 1 desk

BuildMechanicsWhat decides it
LP portfolio pricingRoll NAVs forward, project cash flows on a pacing curve, discount at target return, quote as % of NAV.Mark staleness, unfunded commitments, pacing. Buyout books clear high-80s to mid-90s % of NAV; venture lower.
Continuation vehicleA single-asset LBO underwrite + a 3-party waterfall: rolling LPs, cash-electing LPs, new lead; GP carry crystallizes and resets.The lead's underwrite and the price-vs-NAV fairness optics; whether the GP rolls its carry.
Deferred bidsPay 50% now, 50% in 18 months: leverage disguised as price.Restate to present value before comparing: 97% half-deferred can lose to 93% cash.
Co-investmentThe sponsor's own LBO, fee-free and carry-free, decided in 2 to 4 weeks.Selection: which deals sponsors actually show you, and moving fast enough to matter.
GP stakesValue the manager: FRE on a durable multiple + carry by vintage discounted + balance sheet at book.The next flagship's size: fundraising trajectory moves FRE more than anything.

Carry-along math: a 1.6x target over ~5-year pacing ≈ mid-teens IRR; every 5 points of NAV discount ≈ 1 to 1.5 points of IRR; an 18-month deferral on half the price ≈ 3 to 4 points.
