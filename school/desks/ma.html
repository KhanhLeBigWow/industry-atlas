3 The roles: who does what

On the banking team

Analyst→Associate→VP→MD
AnalystThe model (merger, DCF, comps), the CIM pages, the data room, the buyer grids.
AssociateChecklists and quality control; first drafts of everything; diligence traffic.
VPRuns the process day to day: bid instructions, management meetings, negotiation support.
MDThe relationship and the price talk; wins the mandate; manages the board.

Across the table

Corp dev·Sponsor deal team·Counsel·Arb desk
Corp devThe strategic buyer's inside team: sourcing, synergy cases, integration planning.
Sponsor teamThe LBO underwrite, financing stack, 100-day plan: speed is their bid's currency.
CounselReps, MACs, termination fees: risk allocation between signing and closing.
Merger arbPost-announcement, the market's referee: spread = deal-break probability.

4 The technicals

BuildMechanicsWhat decides it
Merger modelSources & uses, purchase accounting (write-ups, intangibles, goodwill, DTLs), synergies net of costs, pro forma EPS.Accretion/dilution across cash/stock/debt mixes; whether the premium survives honest synergy math.
Valuation stackComps + precedents (control premium) + DCF + LBO ability-to-pay, drawn on the football field.Where the offer lands across independent lenses.
Process designBroad auction vs targeted vs negotiated: tension traded against speed and leak risk.Two real bidders beat 20 tourists.
StructuresCash/stock elections, collars, earnouts/CVRs, tender vs one-step.Every structure allocates signing-to-closing risk.
