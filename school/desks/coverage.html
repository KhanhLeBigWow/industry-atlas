C.1 What a coverage banker actually does

Three jobs wearing one title: relationships (know every CEO, CFO, and board in the sector, and be the first call when something moves), ideas (the pitch: who should buy whom, what to divest, when to refinance: coverage manufactures the deal flow the product desks execute), and sector judgment (which multiple, which buyers are real, what the cycle allows).

The knowledge stack of any coverage seat, bottom to top: the industry's economics (value chain, who captures margin), its KPIs (the 5 numbers the sector actually trades on), its valuation dialect (which lens and why: the valuation atlas), its player map (every company, every sponsor active in the space), and its live tape (deals, earnings, regulation this quarter). Each dossier below carries exactly that stack: structure, KPIs, valuation kit, players, newsroom, risk register.
