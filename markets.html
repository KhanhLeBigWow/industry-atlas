The market map

The whole economy on one screen

Every industry in the atlas as a tile: clustered by sector, sized by global market size, brightness showing profile depth. Click any tile to open its full profile. The finviz view of the real economy, not just the stock market.

Size by

Tile brightness: bright = full profile, medium = profile, dim = stub awaiting research. Market sizes are authored estimates with vintages (see each profile); stubs carry a small placeholder size so everything stays visible. Phase 3 wires live data into the sizes.
