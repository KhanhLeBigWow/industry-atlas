The market map

The whole economy on one screen

Three views of one map: every industry sized by market size, the same industries by how connected they are, or every tracked company sized by market cap and colored by today's move. Click any tile to open its full profile.

View

How to read it: big tiles = industries (brightness = profile depth: bright full, medium profile, dim stub; sizes are authored estimates with vintages). The small ticker chips inside a tile are that industry's largest companies by market cap: click a chip to open the company's full profile, click anywhere else on the tile for the industry. Companies view: boxes sized by market cap; with a free Finnhub key saved below, boxes recolor by today's % move (green/red saturates at ±4%, the finviz convention).

The compact ticker wall: daily % moves for the whole company universe, powered by your own free Finnhub key (stored only in this browser). This is also where you save or forget the key that lights up the Companies view above.
